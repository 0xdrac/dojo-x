Most people book a trial, then spend three days dreading it. We get it. Walking into a room full of people who hit things for fun is intimidating. So here is exactly what happens, so nothing surprises you.

0:00 · Check in

Arrive ten minutes early. Give the front desk your name and phone number; your trial is already on the list. You’ll sign a short waiver on a tablet, get a locker key and borrow any gear you need.

0:05 · Warm-up

Ten minutes of skipping, shadowboxing and mobility. Go at your own pace. Nobody is watching you, they are all too busy catching their breath.

“Every black belt in this building was once the most nervous person in the room.”— SENSEI MEERA DESAI

0:15 · Technique

The coach demonstrates one or two techniques, like a jab-cross or a basic takedown. You’ll pair up with a regular who knows the drill. They will go slow, we promise.

TIPEat something light 90 minutes before class. Training on a full stomach is a mistake you only make once.

0:45 · Rounds & cool-down

A few timed rounds on pads or bags, then stretching. Afterwards, the coach will suggest the right level and batch for you. No hard sell, but yes, you will want to come back.
