01 / THE STORY

From four bags
to forty pros.

In 2013, Arjun Rathore came home from a fight career across Southeast Asia and couldn’t find a single gym in Ghaziabad that taught MMA properly. So he built one, in a rented basement in Vasundhara, with gear he’d carried back in his luggage.

Word travelled. Boxers came for sparring, parents came asking about karate for their kids, and the basement ran out of room. In 2017 Dojo X moved to Sai Complex: 6,000 square feet, a full-size cage and a ring.

Today seven disciplines run under one roof, with 1,800 members aged 5 to 62. Some of them fight for titles. Most just want to feel strong. Both get the same coaching.
